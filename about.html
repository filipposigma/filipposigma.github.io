Filippos Saltsidis is a London-based UX/UI designer with more than fifteen years of experience in graphic, brand and digital design. I started in print and packaging, ran my own studio, and moved steadily into websites, interfaces and design systems, working with clients and teams in Greece, Berlin and London.

I work best at the start of a project, when the problem isn't clear yet. Before I open Figma, I try to understand who the product is for, what they're trying to do and where they get stuck. Then I turn that into structure: clear hierarchy, simple flows and components a team can reuse and build.
